## Context

Built at MOVD as part of the embedded systems team, across multiple KiCad revisions of an ESP32
product.

## What I did

- Designed a **custom ESP32 PCB with an integrated OLED display**, integrating power regulation,
  IO, and test points.
- **Improved system efficiency ~15%** through power-rail and component-selection changes, and cut
  part count via rail consolidation.
- Ran **bring-up, functional validation, and failure isolation** on prototype assemblies with
  oscilloscopes and logic analyzers to resolve hardware defects before release.
