## Context

Work on the SCU Formula SAE electrical team, integrating sensing and power distribution into the
vehicle.

## What I did

- Built **low-voltage signal routing and power distribution** for the vehicle temperature and
  pressure sensing subsystems.
- Analyzed **grounding strategy, fuse protection, and connector current ratings** across the
  vehicle.
- Designed and validated vehicle-grade harnesses to maintain signal integrity on runs to the
  driver display module, sizing conductors and connectors to survive vibration and dynamic load.
